## Challenge

The storefront needed to present products clearly while keeping the route to purchase short. The main risk was adding too much visual weight around the product instead of helping users compare and decide.

## Approach

I treated the product card, filters, and checkout summary as the core system. Each responsive breakpoint keeps the same decision order, so the experience remains predictable from mobile to desktop.

## Result

The sample project demonstrates a practical commerce structure with room for real media, case-study details, and implementation notes.
