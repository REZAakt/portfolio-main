## Challenge

The goal was to turn scattered operational data into a single interface that could be scanned quickly during a busy workday. The product needed to support repeated use without feeling heavy or noisy.

## Approach

I started with the primary decisions users needed to make, then designed the screen hierarchy around those decisions. Metrics, filters, and actions were grouped so the interface could stay dense while still feeling calm.
