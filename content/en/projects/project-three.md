## Challenge

Educational products need structure that can scale. The interface had to support lessons, progress, and long-form content without making the learner feel lost.

## Approach

I separated the experience into clear learning states: discovery, active lesson, progress review, and next action. This made the content model easier to maintain and the UI easier to navigate.
